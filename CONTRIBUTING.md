# Contributing

The Long March is curated by feeling, transition value, and narrative function. A track being good—or historically appropriate—is not enough by itself.

## Before proposing a track

Confirm:

1. The exact track and artist exist on Spotify.
2. The proposal identifies a likely chapter or transition role.
3. The track adds something the current playlist lacks.
4. The proposal is not based only on genre, popularity, or lyrical theme.

## Track proposal format

- Artist:
- Track:
- Spotify URL:
- Suggested chapter:
- Best transition into it:
- Best transition out of it:
- Why it earns a place:
- Possible track it replaces:

## Review expectations

Candidates are scored for atmosphere, march energy, vocal power, emotional peak, transition value, and replayability. Excellent tracks may still be rejected when they damage pacing or duplicate an existing role.

## Release changes

Playlist changes should update:

- The relevant CSV in `playlists/`
- `CHANGELOG.md`
- Artist review notes when applicable
- Hall of Fame only when explicitly approved

Do not silently correct or replace unresolved metadata. Preserve the original entry and document the resolution.
